Hobbies

Reading Books

I remember spending countless hours in my room reading adventure novels under my blanket with a flashlight.

My favorite memory was when I discovered a hidden book collection in my grandparents' attic and spent the entire summer reading through them.

Drawing and Painting

I used to draw colorful pictures of imaginary creatures and paste them all over my bedroom walls.

One special memory is creating a huge mural on cardboard with my best friend that depicted our dream fantasy world.

Toys

Building Blocks

I would spend hours constructing elaborate castles and cities with my colorful building blocks.

My proudest moment was building a replica of a spaceship that was taller than me and showing it to everyone who visited.

Remote Control Car

I loved racing my red remote control car around the backyard and creating obstacle courses.

The most memorable day was when I organized a neighborhood racing competition with all my friends and their RC cars.
